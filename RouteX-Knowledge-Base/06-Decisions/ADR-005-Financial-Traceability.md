# ADR-005: Financial Traceability and No Double Counting

- **Status:** Accepted principle; detailed formulas TBD
- **Sources:** [Financial Rules](../01-Requirements/Business-Rules-and-Financial-Calculations.md); Letter of Agreement

## Decision
Delivery-level and company-level profitability must be distinct. Every result must be traceable to source entries. Expenses must not be counted twice in company-level totals. Do not automatically allocate overhead without an approved rule. The ₱500/hour example is not a fixed universal rate; additional-hour charges are variable.

## Consequences
Calculation rules require RRLI-approved worked examples before automation. Dashboard, reports, truck profiles, and exports must share the same calculation logic.
