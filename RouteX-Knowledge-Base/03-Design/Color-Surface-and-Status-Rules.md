# Color, Surface, and Status Rules

Use restrained accent colors for statuses and maintain sufficient contrast. Avoid using color as the only indication of status. Use clear text labels and consistent status semantics.

Exact palette values, semantic tokens, dark-mode mappings, and approved status taxonomy must be sourced from the current design blueprint. The prototype contains sample statuses that may conflict with the completed-delivery-only workflow; do not copy unapproved Pending/In Transit statuses into production.
