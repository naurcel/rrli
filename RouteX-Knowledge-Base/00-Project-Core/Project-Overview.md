# Project Overview

## Identity
- **Product name:** RouteX
- **System name:** RouteX: Profitability Analysis System
- **Client:** Redeemed Route Logistics Inc. (RRLI)
- **System type:** Internal web-based profitability analysis system for a trucking/logistics business.

## Purpose
RouteX consolidates delivery, truck, employee/crew, revenue, expense, compensation-summary, and Proof of Delivery (POD) records so authorized personnel can retrieve records and evaluate profitability at delivery, truck, and company levels. The central goal is to make the company result traceable to the underlying records while avoiding double-counting expenses.

## Operating model
RouteX is for post-delivery recordkeeping. Staff enter and update information; the system organizes records, calculates approved metrics, retrieves data, and generates reports. It is not a live GPS tracking or live dispatch system.

## Main capabilities
- Completed delivery records and related revenue/expenses
- Fleet and truck history
- Company operating expenses not assigned to a specific delivery
- Employee/crew reference information and compensation summaries
- POD upload and retrieval
- Search, filters, reports, and profitability analysis
- Authentication, role-based permissions, and activity/audit logs

## Important limitations
No live GPS, tracking-device integration, dispatch, route optimization, customer portal, full accounting replacement, tax filing, full payroll engine, attendance, payslips, automatic bank payments, inventory management, or predictive maintenance unless separately approved in writing.
