# Problem Statement and Objectives

## Problem context
Project documents describe RRLI's current processes as relying substantially on spreadsheets, basic bookkeeping, manual communication, and separate documentation methods for operational and financial information. This makes consolidated retrieval and profitability review more difficult.

## Objectives
1. Centralize completed-delivery, truck, client, crew, expense, compensation-summary, and POD records.
2. Improve retrieval using identifiers such as delivery ID, truck plate, driver, client, transaction date, and date range.
3. Support delivery-level, truck-level, and company-level profitability review.
4. Keep delivery-attributable expenses distinct from company operating expenses and avoid counting the same expense more than once.
5. Generate printable/exportable operational and financial reports.
6. Restrict access according to approved roles and preserve audit history for important changes.

**Source note:** Summarized from the SRS and Letter of Agreement. Exact metrics and financial formulas remain subject to approved business rules. See [Issues and TODOs](../08-Logs/Issues-and-TODOs.md).
