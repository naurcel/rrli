# Entity Relationship Diagram

**Status: conceptual only; final ERD TBD.**

The data model must support traceability from company result to truck and delivery, then to underlying revenue/expense entries. It must preserve historical references when trucks or employees are deactivated, and preserve audit/correction history.

Do not treat a source-pack conceptual ERD image or candidate entity list as a final approved schema without explicit review. Create the final ERD after the financial attribution rules and entity lifecycle decisions are resolved.
