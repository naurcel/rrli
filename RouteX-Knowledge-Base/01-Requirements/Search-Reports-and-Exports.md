# Search, Reports, and Exports

## Search and retrieval
Search/filter criteria supported in project materials include delivery ID, truck plate, driver, client, transaction date, and date ranges. Available criteria should reflect actual fields and role permissions. Historical records must remain retrievable after trucks, employees, or users are deactivated.

## Reports
Expected report categories include:
- Delivery activity
- Revenue and delivery expenses
- Company operating expenses
- Compensation summaries
- Approved profitability metrics at delivery, truck, and company levels

Reports should be printable/exportable as specified in the approved scope. The exact formats, columns, filters, grouping, export limits, and per-role permissions require a verified acceptance checklist.

## Calculation consistency
Reports, dashboard totals, truck profiles, and detail screens must use shared approved financial logic. Export must apply the same authorization rules as the UI and must not leak restricted fields.
