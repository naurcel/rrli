# UAT and Acceptance

UAT should be run with designated RRLI users for Operations, Finance, HR, and management roles, plus review of Admin and Corporate Secretary access. Exact participants and sign-off authority are TBD.

UAT flows:
- Enter and retrieve a completed delivery.
- Attach/retrieve POD.
- Correct or void a record and inspect history.
- Register/deactivate a truck and inspect historical deliveries.
- Record company expense.
- Maintain a compensation summary.
- Search historical records by agreed criteria.
- Generate/print/export each approved report.
- Reconcile delivery, truck, and company profitability against signed-off examples.
- Verify role boundaries and direct access restrictions.

Record date, participant, scenario, expected result, actual result, defect/severity, retest status, and explicit acceptance/rejection. Do not infer acceptance from silence.
